The Last Night at Blackwood Hotel

The Abandoned Dining Room

Location

The dining room looks like it has been frozen in time. Plates and glasses are still sitting on the tables, covered in dust. At the far end of the room, an old telephone rings repeatedly. You walk toward it, but the ringing suddenly stops. A second later, the phone rings again. When you pick it up, you hear Alex whispering. “He's downstairs. Don't trust the man in the photographs. Find the basement.” The call cuts out. Behind you, a kitchen door slowly opens by itself.
