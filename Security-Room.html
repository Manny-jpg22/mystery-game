Security Room

Location

You use the master key to enter a small security room. Dozens of old monitors cover one wall. Most of them are broken, but one screen still works. The camera shows the lobby. You watch yourself standing at the front desk. Then you notice something behind you on the screen. A tall figure is standing in the hallway. You turn around in the real room. Nothing is there. When you look back at the screen, the figure is closer. A note taped beneath the monitor says: “The hotel remembers everyone who enters.”
