The Last Night at Blackwood Hotel

The Long Hallway

Location

The hallway stretches farther than you expected. The wallpaper is peeling from the walls, and old photographs of hotel guests cover both sides. As you walk past them, you notice something strange. Every photograph has the same dark figure standing somewhere in the background. You shine your flashlight at one picture. The figure is suddenly gone. Then you hear footsteps behind you. You turn around, but the hallway is empty. At the end of the hall, you see a staircase leading upstairs and a set of double doors leading into the dining room.
