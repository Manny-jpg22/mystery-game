The Abandoned Hotel

You arrive at the abandoned Blackwood Hotel after receiving a strange voicemail from your missing friend, Alex. The message was only a few seconds long: “I'm still here. Please don't let him find me.” The hotel has been closed for years, but tonight the front doors are unlocked. Your phone has barely any signal, and the last message from Alex keeps replaying in your head. You step toward the entrance. The hotel is completely silent. Where will you go?
